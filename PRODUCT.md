# One Holy Bible Product Context

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

One Holy Bible is a local Mac desktop Bible study app. The primary surface is a bilingual reading page (Chinese Union Version with optional KJV) with verse-linked cards in the margin. A separate workbench remains available for layout, search, and card editing.

## Users

The primary user is a focused Chinese Bible reader on a Mac who wants scripture first, then verse-linked notes, images, and encyclopedic cards without leaving the page.

## Product Principles

- Reading comes first: the bilingual text must be comfortable, stable, and visually central.
- Resources follow the selected verse: the right margin is a reference stream, not a competing document.
- Local-first trust: the interface should suggest durable personal study rather than a disposable web page.
- Quiet craft over decoration: use restrained warm paper, precise spacing, and typographic hierarchy instead of ornamental effects.

## Current Scope

- Reader is the default local surface: CUV text, optional KJV, chapter artwork, and a verse-linked card margin.
- Clicking a margin card expands the full card in place; the thumbnail strip and split handle stay on the reading page.
- The workbench remains reachable from the reader top bar for docks, search, and card maintenance.
- Local layout and reader preferences persist through browser localStorage.

## Anti-References

- Default Vite/Tauri starter UI.
- SaaS landing-page cards and oversized hero treatment.
- High-saturation blue or purple dashboards.
- Decorative religious theming that distracts from reading.
- Dense raw admin UI with heavy borders around every element.
