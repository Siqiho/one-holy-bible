## Limits

This is public-source and asset publication, not hosted-site deployment or signed desktop packaging. No new desktop installer was built. The checks verify the selected data projection and representative runtime paths, not a fresh editorial or rights review of every card and illustration. Private editing services and original source documents remain outside the release.
